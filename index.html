About

I am a senior at The Ohio State University double-majoring in Psychology and Statistics, with a minor in Architectural Studies. My research interests relate to the cognitive biases and social-psychological processes behind attitudes and decision-making. I currently work as a research assistant in the Social Problems and Action (SPA) Lab and the Cognition and Brain Circuitry (CBC) Lab here at OSU. Separately, I'm completing an honors thesis on privilege definitions and identity management strategies. I plan to pursue a Ph.D. in Behavioral Marketing and Consumer Behavior to further connect my studies and research to real-world scenarios.
